Common questions

How do I connect? Install the free MyAgent server on your Windows PC, then enter its address in the app (for example your Tailscale address).

Can I try it without a server? Yes, tap "Try the demo server" on the first screen.

Where is my data? On your own computer. See the privacy policy.
