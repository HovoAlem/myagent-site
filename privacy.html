MyAgent Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

MyAgent is a client for the MyAgent server that you run on your own computer. The app does not have its own cloud and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores

On your iPhone: the address of your MyAgent server and your sign-in session. Your chats, files, agent settings and logins are stored on your own MyAgent server, not by us.

Microphone and speech

Used only when you start a voice chat; audio goes to your own MyAgent server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your iPhone's push token is sent to your own MyAgent server so it can notify you.

AI providers

Your MyAgent server may send messages to an AI model provider you configure (for example OpenRouter). That is controlled by you on your server.

Children

MyAgent is not directed to children under 13.
